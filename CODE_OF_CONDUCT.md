# Code of Conduct

We want collaboration on this research to be respectful, constructive, and open to everyone.

**Expected:** critique ideas, not people; assume good faith; credit others' work; give and accept feedback graciously.

**Not acceptable:** harassment or discrimination of any kind, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and plagiarism or misrepresenting authorship.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and contributions that violate this, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do. Report concerns privately to the maintainer email listed in `CITATION.cff` or the repository profile.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
